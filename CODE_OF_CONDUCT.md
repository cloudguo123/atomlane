# Community conduct

We want this project to be technically rigorous and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal attributes.
- Be specific, reproducible, and respectful when reporting failures.
- Do not publish another person's private project data, credentials, prompts, traces, or logs.
- Do not pressure maintainers or contributors to weaken safety boundaries for a benchmark number.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
